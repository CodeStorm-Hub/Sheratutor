## Key Strategic Pillars

| Pillar | Focus | Target Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Zero Student Cost** | Democratized EdTech access | 100% free forever for all SSC & HSC candidates |
| **National Board Grounding** | NCTB Curriculum fidelity | 57 textbooks cataloged and ingested with 0 hallucination |
| **Instant Handwriting Evaluation** | Multimodal Vision AI | Grading within 3.5s with step-by-step rubric breakdown |
| **Institutional Sustainability** | School & Board B2B SaaS | Recurring licensing revenue from schools and coaching centers |
