## 1. Quick Analogy (Explain Like I'm 10)

Imagine someone handed you a thick, heavy book full of scary math and physics formulas and said:  
*"Memorize this page, or you’ll fail your exam!"* 😴

**Now imagine this instead:**

You open your screen, and you walk into a **giant interactive video game laboratory**:

* 🚗 **Physics (Speed & Motion):** You don't just memorize $F = ma$. You get a little car with a speed slider, and you press the gas pedal to see what happens when you brake or push harder.
* 🧪 **Chemistry (Atoms & Molecules):** You don't just read about chemical bonds. You grab atoms like **LEGO bricks**, snap them together, and watch them form molecules in front of your eyes.
* 🔢 **Math (Square Roots & Fractions):** You don't just write numbers on paper. You play a detective puzzle where you drop mystery numbers into treasure chests to uncover why $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.

**Why is it called a Playground?**  
Because on a playground, you don't read a manual—**you hop on and try it yourself**. When you *play* with a concept and see it move, your brain remembers it forever without even trying to memorize it!

## 2. Product Description

**SheraTutor Playground** (`/dashboard/playground/v2`) is an interactive discovery environment engineered specifically for **Secondary School Certificate (SSC / Class 9–10)** and **Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC)** students in Bangladesh.

Rather than relying on passive reading or rote memorization, students manipulate concepts hands-on:
1. **Interactive Physics Simulations:** Adjust force, mass, and friction to visualize Newton's laws, optical ray paths, and electrical circuits.
2. **Dynamic Chemistry Models:** Assemble electron shells, inspect periodic trends, and balance stoichiometric reactions visually.
3. **Mathematical Proof Sandboxes:** Step through geometric proofs and irrational number demonstrations frame-by-frame.
4. **Authentic Board Rubrics:** Built-in warnings about **"Examiner Traps"**—the exact step errors and unit omissions where board examiners deduct marks.

## 3. The 5-Step Learning Framework (Inside Every Guidebook)

Every chapter inside the Playground follows a structured pedagogical flow:

```
[ Step 1: Concept Tree ] ➔ [ Step 2: Interactive Sandbox ] ➔ [ Step 3: Pattern Decoder ] ➔ [ Step 4: Board Trap Alerts ] ➔ [ Step 5: Rapid Board Quiz ]
```

1. **Step 1 — The Concept Universe (শ্রেণিবিন্যাস ও ধারণা):**  
   Interactive hierarchical tree connecting theoretical concepts to real-world applications and textbook syllabus milestones.
2. **Step 2 — Proof Detective & Visual Sandbox (স্যান্ডবক্স ও প্রমাণ):**  
   Tactile SVG experiments, ray-tracing optics, and vector force simulators where students adjust inputs and watch real-time reactions.
3. **Step 3 — Pattern Decoder (গোপন সূত্র ও অ্যালগরিদম):**  
   Live algorithmic tools (e.g., Recurring Decimal Decoder, Mole Calculation Solver, Reaction Balancer) that demystify textbook calculations.
4. **Step 4 — The "Red Line" Board Traps (পরীক্ষকের ফাঁদ ও সাবধানতা):**  
   Direct highlights of the common pitfalls where students lose $0.5$ to $2.0$ marks in Creative Questions (CQ) (e.g., missing SI units, incorrect vector directions, sign flips).
5. **Step 5 — Rapid Board Quiz & Rubrics (ঝটপট বোর্ড কুইজ):**  
   Instant micro-quizzes calibrated to authentic NCTB board question patterns with step-by-step scoring feedback.
