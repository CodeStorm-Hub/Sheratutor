Model Customization: SFT, RFT & DPO in Microsoft Foundry

Solving the two biggest AI bottlenecks in SheraTutor: handwritten bilingual OCR and rigorous board rubric grading.

Reinforcement Fine-Tuning

1. Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)

Handwritten OCR

Target: Llama 3.2 11B Vision or GPT-4o-mini fine-tuning for Bengali handwriting.

  • Dataset Preparation:
    Pairs of handwritten student exam pages × verified verbatim transcription JSONs.
  • Solves Cursive Bengali & Math:
    Eliminates OCR character drops in Bengali conjuncts (যুক্তবর্ণ) and mixed math subscripts ($v_1, a_t$).
Target Model: GPT-4o-mini / Llama 3.2 Vision

2. Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT)

Reasoning & Rubrics

Target: Teaching the model to grade with unyielding consistency like an official board examiner.

  • Reward Functions:
    Positive reward (+1.0) when deductions cite the exact NCTB rubric rule; negative penalty (-2.0) for hallucinated marks.
  • Mathematical Rigor:
    Enforces intermediate step unit checks ($\text{ms}^{-1}$, $\text{J}$) without forgiving missing symbols.
Azure AI Foundry RFT Engine

3. Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)

Socratic Tone

Target: Aligning the Socratic Tutor Agent's tone with top human educators.

  • Preference Pairs:
    Senior teachers rate two AI tutor responses: preferred (guiding question) vs rejected (direct answer dump).
  • Cultural Resonance:
    Optimizes natural, warm Bengali phrasing and relatable local physics analogies.
Teacher Preference Calibration
